## 💰 MONETIZATION PROGRAMS (Complete Guide)

### 1. Creator Rewards Program (CRP)
**URL:** `/article/creator-rewards-program`
- **What:** Earn rewards for original videos **over 1 minute** long
- **Eligibility:** 18+, 10K followers, 100K views/30 days, eligible countries
- **Countries:** US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil
- **Reward types:**
  - **Standard Reward** — base reward per qualified view
  - **Additional Reward** — bonus for Well-crafted + Engaging + Specialized content
- **Qualified views:** ≥5 sec watch time, unique per account, no fraud/paid
- **Ineligible content:** Unoriginal, low quality, ads, clickbait, slideshows, Duets, Stitches, Photo Mode
- **Violation thresholds:** 5 video violations in 30 days OR 5 total account violations = permanent ineligibility
- **Payment:** Via PayPal, Pix, or bank. Processed within 72 hours.
- **Appeal window:** 80 days for video, 30 days for account disqualification

#### High-Quality Content Framework (for Additional Reward)
```
1. Well-crafted   → Attention to detail, professional preparation & editing
2. Engaging       → Sparks conversations, resonates, builds community
3. Specialized    → Deep expertise in a niche, unique valuable perspective
```
> ⚡ High-quality creators have 72% more watch time per view, 42% lead over 10K+ follower accounts, and 40x greater follower growth

### 2. Subscription
**URL:** `/article/subscription`
- **What:** Monthly recurring revenue from subscribers who unlock exclusive perks
- **Eligibility:** 18+, 10K followers, 100K video views/calendar month
- **Price tiers:** \$2.99 – \$99.99/month (default \$5.99)
- **Revenue split:** 70% to creator (after app store fees); up to 90% with bonus
- **Bonus (+20%):** Post first sub-only video (new) OR 3 sub-only videos/month (returning)
- **Perks available:** Exclusive videos, Subscriber badges, personalized stickers (up to 35), custom perks
- **Setup path:** Profile → TikTok Studio → Monetization → Subscription

### 3. TikTok One (formerly Creator Marketplace + Creative Challenge)
**URL:** `/article/tiktok-one`
- **What:** Brand collaboration platform — create authentic ads for brand campaigns
- **Posting options:** On your profile, brand's profile, or as paid ads
- **Benefit:** More flexible posting + faster payment management

### 4. TikTok Shop (Affiliate)
**URL:** `/article/tiktok-shop`
- **What:** Earn affiliate rewards from products sold via videos, LIVE, or storefront
- **Eligibility:** 18+, active TikTok community
- **Discovery paths for buyers:**
  1. Product link pinned to video
  2. Creator's store page on profile
  3. LIVE shopping sessions
- **Key advantage:** No redirects — viewers buy without leaving TikTok
- **Strategy tool:** Creator Search Insights for finding right products

### 5. LIVE Monetization
**URL:** `/article/live-monetization`

#### LIVE Gifts
- Viewers send virtual Gifts → creator earns Diamonds → converted to cash rewards
- Activate via Profile → Settings → Balance → toggle LIVE Gifts ON
- Withdrawal: PayPal or bank account

#### LIVE Match
- Friendly competition between creators during LIVE
- Modes: **Solo (1v1)** or **Team (1v2, 1v3, 2v2)**
- Match Power-ups:
  - 🥊 **Boosting Glove** — multiplies gift points x5 for 30 sec
  - 🌫️ **Magic Mist** — hides your points from opponent for 30 sec
  - ⏱️ **Time Maker** — extends match by up to 10 sec
  - 🔨 **Stun Hammer** — triggers hammer effects on opponent
  - **No.2 & No.3 Boosters** — double points for top 2-3 contributors

#### Gift Gallery
- Weekly task (resets every Monday 12AM local time)
- Viewers send specific Gifts to complete tasks → earn titling rights
- Title gifter gets flame effect + prominent label in LIVE room

#### LIVE Rankings & Campaigns
- Weekly top 99 rankings based on Diamonds received in past 7 days
- Top 20 creators each week get prizes
- Hosts ranked 4-10: featured on "Explore More" + special thumbnail label
- LIVE Campaigns: Special events with extra rewards; opt-in/out any time

### 6. Video Gifts
- Viewers send Gifts in comments on regular videos
- Creator receives Diamonds automatically
- Eligibility: video must be enabled for gifts

### 7. Work With Artists (WWA)
- Create videos featuring new songs/musicians
- Rewards based on content performance
- Frequent participants can earn guaranteed rewards

### 8. Effect House Creator Rewards
**URL:** `/article/effect-house-rewards`
- **Eligibility:** Gold/Platinum/Diamond badge + 5 effects published + 3 effects each used in 1K+ qualified videos
- **90-day window system:** Effects must hit usage thresholds within 90 days of publishing
- **Thresholds by category:**
  | Effect Type | Videos Needed |

## 🎬 CONTENT CREATION BEST PRACTICES

### Getting Started on TikTok
- Download app → Sign up (email/phone/3rd party) → Customize profile
- For users under 16: auto-set to Private
- FYP (For You Page): Users spend 96% of time here
- Key icons: Profile, Heart (like), Comment, Bookmark, Share, Spinning disc (sound)

### 8 Tips for Successful TikTok Creators
1. **Find your niche** — Authenticity > chasing trends; write a personal mission statement
2. **Consistency over quantity** — Quality content calendar beats daily low-quality uploads
3. **Create informative in-depth content** — Solve problems, bust myths, share hacks, teach step-by-step
4. **Choose the right aspect ratio** — Vertical (default) OR Horizontal (Full Screen mode for landscape)
5. **Pay attention to filming details** — Stable camera, great lighting, multiple angles
6. **Elevate editing skills** — Hook in first 3 seconds, fast-paced cuts, captions, transitions
7. **Dig into Analytics** — Focus on watch time, drop-off points, audience retention
8. **Use TikTok Studio** — One-stop shop for editing, scheduling, analytics, bulk upload

## 🛡️ COMMUNITY GUIDELINES OVERVIEW

**URL:** `/article/community-guidelines`
- Content may be: Removed, Age-restricted (adults only), or Reduced in FYP (not promoted broadly)

- **Moderation process:** Visit TikTok's Safety Center for full details

### Account Standing & Ineligibility Triggers
Content/accounts become ineligible when they contain:
- **Unoriginal** — copied content, minimal original input
- **Low quality** — split screens, meaningless reactions, slide videos, low-res images
- **Advertising** — personal/business contact info in profile
- **Clickbait** — content primarily designed to get follows/likes with no value
- **Inappropriate** — sexually suggestive, excessive body exposure
- **Disturbing** — shocking/graphic scenes
- **Security issues** — artificial engagement, harassment, suspicious activity
- **Government/Political** accounts — not eligible for monetization programs
- **Business accounts** — not eligible for CRP or Subscription
