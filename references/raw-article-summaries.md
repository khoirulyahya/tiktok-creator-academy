## Article 1: Creator Rewards Program (17M views)
**Fetched content summary:**
The Creator Rewards Program (CRP) lets creators earn from original videos over 1 minute. 
Launched with Spring 2025 update: "Additional Reward" for high-quality content.

Key eligibility criteria:
- Countries: US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil
- 18+ years old
- 10,000 followers minimum
- 100,000 video views in last 30 days
- Personal accounts only (no Business, Government, Political accounts)
- Account in good standing

Enrollment steps:
1. Profile → Menu (☰)
2. Select TikTok Studio → Creator Rewards Program
3. Apply (response within 3 days)

Ineligibility triggers:
- Unoriginal content
- Low quality (split screens, reactions, slide videos)
- Ads/contact info in profile
- Clickbait
- Inappropriate/disturbing content
- Security issues/suspicious activity
- 5 video violations in 30 days → temporary ineligibility
- 5 total account violations → permanent ineligibility
- Business accounts, Government/Political accounts

Qualified view criteria:
- ≥5 seconds watch time
- "Not interested" taps excluded
- Multiple views from same account = 1 unique view
- Search views require ≥30 seconds watch time
- Must come from CRP-eligible countries
- No ads/paid promotions

Appeal process:
- Video appeal: 80-day window; In-app Inbox → notification → Submit an appeal
- Account appeal: 30-day window

Withdrawal:
- Via PayPal, Pix, or bank account
- Processed within 72 hours (financial institutions may add delay)

Tax: Creator responsible for local tax obligations. Check status in TikTok dashboard.

Invoice download:
Profile → Menu → Balance → Monthly Earnings → Transactions → specific transaction → Download

## Article 5: LIVE Monetization (6.4M views)

### LIVE Gifts
- Toggle: Profile → Settings → LIVE Gifts
- Viewers send virtual gifts → appear on screen → Diamonds collected at LIVE end
- More gifts = higher LIVE ranking

### LIVE Match
Solo (1v1) or Team (1v2, 1v3, 2v2) competition modes.
Power-ups (distributed to top 3 contributors of winning team):
- Boosting Glove: x5 points for 30 sec
- Magic Mist: hides your points 30 sec
- Time Maker: +10 sec to match
- Stun Hammer: hammer effects on opponent
- No.2 & No.3 Boosters: double points for those ranked contributors

### Gift Gallery
- Weekly task (Monday reset, 12AM local)
- Viewers complete gift tasks → earn title in your LIVE room
- Title gifter: flame effect on their gifts in your room
- Progress resets every 7 days

### Gaming Ranking
- Top 99 game creators per region
- Based on Diamonds from past 7 days
- Updated weekly

### Guessing Game
- YES/NO questions; viewers bet game points
- Correct answer: bonus points; Wrong: lose points
- Can vote for creator using game points

### Gift Vote
- Updated version of Gift Poll
- Viewers vote on questions by sending Gifts during LIVE

## Article 6: Going LIVE (6.3M views)

Requirements: 18+ (or age of majority in country)

How to start:
1. Tap Create icon
2. Swipe to LIVE
3. Choose image + title
4. Tap "Go LIVE"

Controls during LIVE:
- Flip camera
- Add effects
- Filter comments
- Appoint up to 20 moderators

Best practices:
- Duration: 60-80 min >> 10-20 min
- Post video before LIVE for FYP discovery boost
- Test internet connection beforehand
- One stable camera angle
- Good lighting
- Plan content ahead

Inactive creator rule: TikTok closes LIVE if host is inactive and fails verification.

Weekly Rankings:
- Top 20 → prizes
- Rank 4-10 → "Explore More" feature + thumbnail label
- Opt out: LIVE Screen → Settings → Rankings
or During LIVE: More → Admin Settings → Rankings

LIVE Campaigns:
- Special TikTok-hosted events; look for banners on LIVE page
- Each campaign page has participation rules, rewards, T&C
- Opt out: "Opt-Out" button on campaign page

Rules:

- Max 100 videos added at once
- Each video appears in only ONE playlist
- Name limit: 25 characters

- Rename: Menu (…) → Edit Playlist → Edit icon

- Remove: Menu (…) → Edit Playlist → Remove videos

Default video order: oldest to newest (by when added)

Viewer experience:
- Link to Playlist shows on videos
- Playlist Details page with swipe-through (like FYP)
